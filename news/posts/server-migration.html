🚧 Scheduled Downtime & Major Upgrades 🚧

The Techy Bechy server will be going offline for a period of time between September 20th and the first few days of October. This downtime is necessary as we migrate the entire server to a new setup and prepare for several exciting improvements.

This isn’t just routine maintenance — it’s a big move that will affect almost every part of the server. While things are offline, you won’t be able to connect, but rest assured the work being done behind the scenes will make your future gameplay much smoother and more fun.

Here’s what you can expect once we’re back online:

  • 🔼 Higher player limits — more room for friends, events, and chaos without the lag.
  • 🛡️ Better griefing prevention — stronger tools and protections so your builds stay safe.
  • 🗺️ BlueMap updates — the interactive world map will load faster and give you a better overview of our worlds.
  • ⚡ Performance boosts — optimizations under the hood to reduce lag spikes and improve tick rates.
  • 📦 Server migration — moving everything onto a cleaner, more powerful environment for long-term stability.

During this downtime, we encourage everyone to stay tuned for announcements. We’ll be posting progress updates, behind-the-scenes notes, and sneak peeks of the changes as they go live.

You can follow all the detailed updates right here on our news page: 👉 techybechy.alberttalkstech.com/news

We know it’s tough to wait, but this short break will pay off in the long run. The new setup means a faster, more reliable Techy Bechy experience for everyone — and it sets us up for even bigger features in the future.
