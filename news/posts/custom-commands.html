Custom Commands on Techy Bechy

Here’s a quick guide to the custom commands available on our server:

Available Commands

  • 🏠 /lobby — Teleport to the lobby!
  • ✨ /tpxp ws — Teleport to spawn using 1 XP level.
  • 🎯 /tpxp p <player> — Teleport to another player for 3 XP levels.
  • 👀 /watchspawn — Toggle watching the spawn area.
  • 📬 /mail — Check, send, and clear your mail.

Use these commands to get around quickly, keep in touch, and make your gameplay smoother. 🚀
